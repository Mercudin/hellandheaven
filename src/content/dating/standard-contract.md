I want someone to serve me.  These are my terms.

This contract was developed because our institutions are broken.  There is very little sense left in dating in the hopes of marriage.  I want someone to serve me, and I want to take very good care of the person who takes on this role, now and after I'm gone.

I have room in my life for someone special.  Someone who wishes to take care of my needs as I take care of hers, recognizing that we both have very different needs and different ways of caring.

I will take on the traditional masculine role of providing and protecting.  I want my partner to feel safe and secure with me always.  In return, I expect my partner to take on the feminine role of caring for me and our home.

In the ideal scenario, my partner won't require money at all.  A partner living with me will be taken care of to the same degree I take care of myself, and will be provided with all the amenities that I myself indulge.

In return, I expect such a partner to take care of me and the home.  Details of that are negotiable, but this will be one of the ways in which you serve.


Standard Contract (friends only)

I developed this contract with a previous partner, and while the edges of it could certainly be modified for a new partner, I believe the core of this is a good starting point.

 - Companion shall be available and in the Room from 10pm - 8am unless we are out together (see The Room for more details)

 - Companion will attend to the needs of the house as determined by mutual negotiation

 - Companion will be provided with all material needs at Costco/Target level (see That Costco Life for more details)

 - Companion will start acruing a percentage of incoming resources after one year of living in the Room, in order to provide a safety net in case of the worst.
