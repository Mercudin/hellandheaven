The Princess Treatment

She was going to help me with my wardrobe and pictures, and ensure there's no red flags anywhere. I read something recently from a woman who wrote that she wanted to hear the story of a man's life, and see in that story how she would fit into it.  This is how I fit her into it for a weekend.

On the first night I took her to my favorite restaurant in downtown Danville.  We split an appetizer and an entree so we could save room for dessert, and walked to the best ice cream in town.  It was a beautiful warm night, and we spent the rest of the evening walking through town, cute neighborhoods, and a bit of the trail before heading home. I wake her up for sex in the morning, which is one of my absolute favorite things to do.  There's nothing quite like waking up horny (and slightly too early) next to someone sexy who is willing to be woken up to get gloriously morning fucked, and then going back to sleep.  The 90 minutes of sleep I get after morning sex is by far the best REM sleep I get. After a fantastic sleepy morning, we meander out into the backyard to enjoy some coffee and sunshine. I get some time in the cold plunge while she uses the hot tub, and we chat away the time until we head out for a late brunch at my favorite steak and eggs place.

We leave and pick up the kids to take them to Great America to ride rollercoasters.  My ex already knows and loves my kids, and the feeling is mutual, so we all go enjoy some upside-down time together.  

I have a bit of a different dynamic when it comes to introducing new partners to my kids.  My ex-wife and I had been poly before we separated, and the kids were used to other close, friendly adults being around.  After separating, I was using a bit more discretion about having new people around my kids, but they didn't like that at all.  They told me in no uncertain terms that they like the women I have around and that I'm more fun when we have company.  I think sometimes they just want someone to show off for, but then I wonder if I don't in some way want the same.

And I should mention here that my kids are different.  Outliers.  The result of that assortative mating we've been warned about.  Curious monsters with sharp minds.  Our parenting philosophy was to answer their questions in slightly more detail than we think they can comprehend at their current age, and we have been routinely surprised at just how much they remember and articulate from these answers.  Recently we've been enjoying some meta-level discussions on how to parent them, as they're old enough to recognize that all toddlers are assholes and you have to somehow ethically train them not to be.  We enjoy discussions with other outliers.

We stop for sushi on the way to taking the kids back home, and return for a night of sitting in a hot tub and looking at the stars.  We return inside for what can only be called a drug-fueled sex bender.

I mostly limit myself to weed and alcohol, and enjoy both of these with sex but I don't overindulge too much in either one.  My expanded palatte includes some hallucinagens, ketamine, MDMA, and GHB, and I partake of these pretty rarely.  But Princess was ready to try some things and do some stuff, and I was on board with this plan. I had read about circling, but never experienced it, and it was a shockingly emotional experience.  The sex and drugs probably helped.

We fall asleep cuddled together, wake up and decide that we really just want to continue doing what we were doing last night, and spend the morning and some of the afternoon out in the sunshine or in bed together, until finally putting some clothes on at 4pm and hitting up a local steakhouse to start the day.

We get some phenomenal steak and ahi tuna, and we're done with dinner by 5pm.  Which is great because that leaves just enough time for a hike that I'd wanted to show her.  The hike is through a canyon very close to my home, and is terrific this time of year as it's shady enough to make it great for a warm day.  We take a moderate dose of over the counter magic mushrooms, and head off for our walk.

The hike is, for lack of a better word, magical.  The time in nature is great, our spirits are high, the mushrooms are just right, and we turn a 80 minute hike into a 3.5 hour tour of rarely explored paths and appreciating nature's beauty.  And I really have to thank Princess for this one.  I was doing this hike a good bit the previous summer, and never took the time to go off the path and explore the place.  She showed me the beauty in the paths not taken.

We returned home for our last night together and... crashed.  I would have wanted more for our last night, but we were both exhausted, and the cuddling was great.  This was a weekend of far more substances than I usually consume, and my body was ready for some recovery time.

When she left, we talked about what I would need in a relationship to be happy, and what kind of woman would be happy with me.  She would not only have to be affectionate, but also comfortable receiving my near limitless affection, which will often lead to sex.  I should find someone who desires that kind of energy from me.

I told her that the ideal woman might be one who wants to be whisked away from her current life, but that in itself is a red flag and I should be wary of such a woman.  She didn't have any advice for me here, and I still haven't resolved this one.
