I would prefer not to have to cause my partner any real pain to get what I want from her.

And that's where I am now.  My life is back on track and I'm ready to date again.  I would love to hear from someone who would attend an RMN party, is curious to experience this Jack character for herself, and is looking for a new possibly primary poly partner.
