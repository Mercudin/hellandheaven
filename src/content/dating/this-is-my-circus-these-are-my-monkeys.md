title: "This is my circus, these are my monkeys"
order: 1
---
I had a spiritual event in 2021 that was so intense that it feels like I woke up in this guy's life, and have been figuring out how I want to live since that happened.  I found myself in a suburban home, married to the Librarian with two kids, and also seeing my long term girlfriend, the Demoness.

I came to the realization that I hadn't been treating the Demoness as well as I should.  She had been my girlfriend for nearly a decade, and she had never put her heart into dating since I was providing most of what she needed in that realm.  I decided that I should do a better job of taking care of this creature who had been good to me for so long, and hatched a plan to take care of her.  I would rent a house for us, start a circus, and have the proceeds of the circus go to taking care of her and that house.  I had been a circus performer for some time, but this was my first time as a ringleader.

It turns out that I'm a fairly good ringleader, and am pretty good at running a circus, but not so good at managing time or relationships.  Or maybe I was simply spread too thin.  I didn't have enough hours in the day to be a good husband, father, boyfriend, and ringleader.  I had a great circus, but a pissed off wife and girlfriend.  Something had to give when the Librarian and Demoness started fighting for my time, and I chose family over the Demoness, which was the beginning of the end of the circus.  I even tried canceling the circus to focus on the relationships, but the damage had already been done.

But I liked running a circus.  When I started dating the Witch, and found she was enthralled with the idea of running a circus with me, I started it back up again.  The circus was still great for the peanut gallery, and we even moved into fancier new digs to accommodate a bigger tent, but my heart wasn't in it.  I wasn't taken with the Witch like I was the Demoness, and I found that I didn't have enough motivation to run a circus if I didn't have a partner I was excited about, and I started to prioritize finding a partner over running the circus.

I had a brief tryst with Cupid, and after finding a lucky coin and wishing for her to find love with it, I was introduced to the Princess.  The Princess is another circus performer, and came to work at my circus, and we fell for each other instantly.  She was a free spirit, and it was easy to pull her out of her own life and install her in mine.  But she didn't belong there.  She was a performer, not a hostess, and we weren't long term compatible.  It was only months before we parted ways.

I was just about ready to give up the circus again at this time.  I didn't need the money, and could always go back to being a performer.  And I wanted the free time to rest and recover and get back to searching for a long term partner.  It was at this time that I met the Angel.

I met her at another circus, and immediately tried to recruit her to mine.  But I didn't just want her to work at my circus, I wanted her for my own.  So I went about wooing her, and it worked.  I pulled her out of her life and into mine, like I had done countless times to others before her.

She worked for my circus for a time, but the circus itself required a change.  I was more focused on my new relationship, and the Witch had lost interest as well, and wasn't doing the same job she used to do for me.  I shut down everything, and moved to a new location with the Angel, and got ready to start a new circus with her.  I finally had something approaching the original model I had with the Demoness.  A circus to host with a partner I loved, which would provide for us everything we needed.

And then, just before opening weekend, the Angel decided we shouldn't be romantic partners anymore. I was devastated in the moment, as I didn't see this one coming, but I also saw all of the incompatibilities that she saw.  I didn't want to acknowledge them, and had an easier time avoiding them as I was focused on restarting the circus with her.  But I saw.  I was about to make the same mistake I had kept making before.  I see now that mixing business and pleasure is a recipe for disaster, and I'm going to stop concocting that same brew.

I have delayed the reopening of the circus, but it will open again soon.  With hired help this time.

And I still very much want a relationship.  I'm good at making money, somewhat lackluster at taking care of myself, and really like having a partner who takes care of me, for whom I provide stability and security.

The problem for me now is not jumping into something unhealthy, rounding up another tepid relationship to something greater just because it gives me the sex and connection I crave.  I am holding out for real connection this time.  I hope to find it when I'm ready.

In the meantime, I've got a circus to run.  If you've read to the end of this, and have no idea what you just read or if any of this is real, I welcome you to reach out.  I even run a training night on Fridays where I teach my kids and anyone else who wants to learn, so if you're an aspiring circus performer looking for an extra gig and you look great in a sequined outfit,  hit me up.  But be warned!  My world is unlike any you've known, and it's kind of easy to get sucked into the circus lifestyle.  And if you wanna be my girl, you'll have to leave the circus behind.
