I'm going out on a limb and trying something new here.  Trying to be the change I want to see in the world.

I want to teach my kids, and especially my son, what it is that I do, which is playing in and hosting a poker game.  I didn't think this was important until recently, but now that I see AI taking over a lot of jobs that I thought would be good for my kids, I want to pivot and teach them something that humans will still value in a world where AI does all the programming.  Specifically, I want to teach them how to deal poker because I think live poker games will persist.

But I need a little help in this endeavor.  They won't quite pay attention the same way if I'm not also teaching this to other adults.  Fortunately, this is also a valuable skill for other adults to have!  I just need some of you to show up occasionally on a Friday night (because that's a night I consistently have the kids) and learn how to deal poker for my game.

And I'm a very good teacher in this arena, with decades of experience.  Not only can I teach you how to be an excellent dealer, but I can also teach you how to play poker if you're interested. This is probably the only game where I could promise to tell you the truth about how to play a hand instead of just telling you that you played it right.  There's an ethical code to real money games, after all, but this is just a training game.

And as a bonus, dealers who show up to training night and show me that they know what they're doing will have priority for shifts at my real money game, which are quite lucrative.

I have no idea if this will work or if I can get Poker Training Night off the ground as a concept.  But I want it to be a friendly thing.  Something where we order door dash and take turns learning to deal and play.  Something I can invite my non-poker friends to join.  Something where my kids are learning and having fun doing it, and you are too.  Let me know if you have any interest in joining this, ever. :P
